About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yong Cui. My research centers on AI for Networking & Networking for AI, with the goal of building more automated, reliable, and efficient networked systems. Specifically, I study LLM-agent-based network configuration and protocol testing, as well as network observability, fault localization, and communication optimization for AI infrastructure.

Research

D-Pingmesh: Dynamic probing & fault localization

2026

Developed control-plane-aware active probing and fault-localization methods for datacenter networks undergoing topology and traffic-engineering changes. Evaluated the methods through simulation and testbed experiments. (ATC 2026 · Accepted)

INTA: Intent-based configuration translation

2024–2026

Designed an agentic framework that decomposes configurations into intents, retrieves target-device configuration procedures, and incrementally verifies generated configurations. Achieved 98.15% syntax and view correctness and an 84.72% command-level match rate on an industrial cross-vendor dataset. (IEEE ICNP 2025)

Protocol testing: From specifications to executable tests

2025–2026

Developed an end-to-end workflow for interpreting protocol specifications, generating test cases, and executing tests on network devices. Structured representations and constrained generation improve completeness and compatibility with practical test environments. (ACM/IRTF ANRW 2025)

Distributed training: Communication optimization across layers

2023–2024

Systematized optimization across parallelization strategies, collective communication libraries, and networks. Proposed a five-layer cross-layer co-design framework and directions for coordinating heterogeneous resources. (IEEE Network 2025)

xNet: Learning to model network performance

2022–2023

Contributed as second author, with responsibility for modeling and experimental evaluation in wide-area-network scenarios. xNet achieved over 100× speedup over packet-level simulators. (IEEE/ACM ToN 2024)
